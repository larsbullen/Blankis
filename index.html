Before you go

  • Ice picks (isdubbar) around your neck
  • Ice pole (isstav) to test ahead
  • Throw rope (kastlina) per group
  • Dry clothes in a sealed bag, in a backpack with a hip belt
  • Phone in a waterproof pouch, and the 112 app
  • Never alone, with 10–20 m between skaters
  • Tell someone your route and when you'll be back
  • Skate guards or light boots for the walking stretches
  • Headlamps, plus spare batteries (cold drains them)
  • Rule of thumb: 5 cm of clear ice per person, 10 cm for a group
